FAQ

Common questions.

Everything you need to know before you send your plans.

Still have a question?

Email admin@navioestimate.com.au — or call 0408 069 002. We reply within 1 business day.

How much does an estimate cost?

$320 per job — one fixed price, paid up front. No hourly rates and no scope creep on the estimate itself.

Is $320 really the price for every job?

Yes. Every job is $320, whatever the type or size of the project. You know exactly what you’ll pay before you send the plans.

When do I pay?

Payment of $320 is made up front, before work on your estimate begins.

What if my plans change?

No problem — changes can be made, and all revisions are included in the $320. Just send us the updated plans.

Do you work Australia-wide?

Yes. We’re based in Sydney and prepare estimates for projects right across Australia, using practical rates built for Australian work.

How long does it take?

Your estimate is delivered in 2–5 business days. We reply within 1 business day of receiving your plans to confirm we’re under way.

What do I need to send you?

Just the plans. Email them to admin@navioestimate.com.au or use the request form. A short note about the job is helpful but not required.

What types of projects do you estimate?

Commercial fitouts (offices, retail, hospitality, medical suites and refurbishments), residential projects (renovations, extensions, new builds and multi-unit work), trade packages, interior design packages, and small works & alterations.

What will I receive?

A complete estimate prepared by our in-house team: scope of works, a trade-by-trade breakdown, measured quantities, line-item rates and pricing, margin and contingency, and a clear list of inclusions and exclusions.

You get it as an editable Excel file plus a clean PDF summary.

Can I use the estimate for tendering?

Yes. Our estimates are built to be used — for pricing jobs and for tenders, not just to look neat on paper.

Who prepares the estimates?

Our in-house estimation team, based in Sydney. Navio Estimate was started after more than two years working in the building and housing industry, so the numbers are built for how jobs really run on site.
